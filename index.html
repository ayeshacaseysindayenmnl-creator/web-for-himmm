My Letter To You

Hi bebe, I don't really have a big fancy opening for this, because honestly, I don't think there are enough perfect words in the world to explain everything I feel for you. I've been thinking about all the little things I want you to know, all the things I sometimes feel but don't always know how to say out loud, and I realized that maybe the best thing I can do is simply write them down. So, bebe, get comfortable because this is probably going to be really long. HAHAHA. I have so much to say about you, about us, about the little moments we've shared, and about all the things I hope we get to experience together someday. I just want you to know that you deserve to hear these things, not just assume them from the way I act. You deserve to know how deeply I appreciate you, how much your presence means to me, and how grateful I am that somehow, in this huge world full of people, our paths crossed and I got the chance to know you, love you, and call you mine. Sometimes I still think about how crazy it is that out of all the people we could have met, somehow it became us. Somehow you're the person I get to talk to, laugh with, annoy, miss, and love. And honestly, I wouldn't trade that for anything. One of the things I love most about us is that our relationship isn't only made up of huge moments. It's made up of tiny things that probably don't seem important to anyone else but mean everything to me. I love the way you check up on me kahit pagod ka na. I love those little "kumain ka na?" messages, the random "ingat ka," the "nakauwi ka na?" and all those simple questions that secretly tell me that you're thinking about me. Sometimes you might think you're just asking a normal question, but to me, it feels like you're saying, "I care about you, so please take care of yourself." I notice those things, bebe. I notice when you remember something I told you once in passing, when you remember a random story I shared, when you remember something I like or something that made me happy. I love knowing that little pieces of me stay in your mind. It makes me feel seen. It makes me feel like I'm important to you, and I hope you know that those little efforts never go unnoticed. Even the smallest things you do can stay with me for the entire day. Sometimes I'll randomly remember something you said or did and catch myself smiling for no reason. That's how much the little things matter to me. I don't need everything to be grand or perfect. Sometimes all I need is that little reminder that you're there, that you're thinking about me, and that somewhere in the middle of your busy day, I crossed your mind. You have no idea how much comfort you bring me, bebe. There are days when everything feels like too much, when my mind is full of thoughts and I don't even know where to start, when I'm tired and don't have the energy to explain what's wrong. Sometimes I don't even need someone to solve anything for me. I don't need perfect advice or some magical answer that suddenly makes everything disappear. Sometimes I just need someone who stays, and somehow, you're that person for me. Just talking to you can make things feel lighter. Even if we're talking about something completely random, even if we're just joking around, even if we're barely saying anything at all, your presence has this way of making everything feel a little less heavy. You don't always have to fix my problems, bebe. You don't have to know exactly what to say. Sometimes just knowing that you're there is enough. There is something about you that makes my mind quiet down. You make me feel like I can breathe when everything feels overwhelming. And I hope you know how special that is to me because not everyone can make me feel that way. Not everyone can make me feel safe enough to just exist without having to explain myself. But with you, somehow I can. You became one of the people I look for when I'm happy, when I'm sad, when something funny happens, when something annoying happens, and even when I have absolutely nothing important to say. I just want to talk to you because talking to you feels like home. I also love that I don't have to pretend around you. I don't have to always be the best version of myself. I can be tired, weird, annoying, clingy, dramatic, quiet, talkative, moody, or completely random, and somehow you still stay. I can show you the sides of myself that aren't always easy to understand, and you don't make me feel like I'm too much. I don't have to carefully choose every word because I'm scared you'll think differently of me. I can laugh at stupid things, say nonsense, complain about little things, get excited over something completely random, and you still somehow entertain me. That kind of comfort is something I don't take lightly. Being able to be completely myself around someone is rare, and being accepted while doing that is even rarer. So thank you, bebe, for letting me be me. Thank you for making me feel like I don't have to be perfect to be loved. Thank you for staying even when I'm difficult. Thank you for understanding me even when I don't understand myself. I hope I can give you the same kind of comfort too. I hope you know that you never have to pretend around me either. You can be tired with me. You can be quiet with me. You can have bad days with me. You can tell me when something is bothering you. You can be completely yourself, because I don't want some perfect version of you. I want you. The real you. Every version of you. We've already been through different kinds of days together, and I know not every day will always be easy. We've had our ups and downs, our misunderstandings, our moments when things felt heavier than usual, and our days when maybe we didn't know exactly how to handle everything. But honestly, those moments have taught me something important about us. They taught me that love isn't just about the days when everything is perfect. Love is also about what we do when things aren't perfect. It's about learning how to understand each other, learning how to communicate, learning when to listen, learning when to apologize, and learning how to choose each other again after difficult moments. And even after all the hard days, you're still the person I want next to me. You're not someone I only want around when everything is easy. You're someone I want on my team. I want you beside me when we're celebrating something beautiful, but I also want you beside me when we're trying to figure things out. I don't expect us to never have problems because we're human, and we're both still learning. But I hope that whenever life gets difficult, we remember that we're supposed to face it together instead of against each other. I want us to be able to look at each other after a hard day and say, "Okay, let's figure this out." I want us to keep choosing understanding over pride, patience over anger, and love over giving up. You honestly make me want to become a better person, and I want you to know that it's not because you ever made me feel like I wasn't enough. It's actually the opposite. Loving you makes me want to grow because I want to become someone who can love you better, understand you better, and give you the kind of love you deserve. I notice how hard you try, even when things don't go the way you want them to. I notice how you still show up even when you're tired. I notice how you care about people, how you do things without always expecting something in return, and how you keep going even when things aren't easy. Sometimes I look at you and think about how much more there is to you than what people see on the outside. There are so many little things about your heart that make me admire you. And I hope you know that I'm proud of you, not only when you succeed, but also when you're simply trying. I'm proud of you on the days when you feel like you're doing great, and I'm proud of you on the days when you feel like you're barely getting through. You don't always have to accomplish something huge for me to be proud of you. Sometimes simply getting through a difficult day is already enough. I hope you never forget that. And honestly, even the boring days with you don't feel boring. That's something I love so much about us. I don't need every day to be some huge adventure. I don't need expensive dates, fancy places, or grand romantic gestures all the time. I could sit beside you while we're both doing absolutely nothing and still be happy. We could be scrolling on our phones, watching random videos, eating together, talking about nonsense, or sitting quietly without saying much, and I'd still think, "I like being here." I like being around you even when nothing special is happening. Maybe that's one of the ways I know what we have is special. Because sometimes the best moments aren't the ones we plan. Sometimes they're the moments where we're just existing beside each other and somehow it feels enough. I want more of those moments with you. More random conversations, more stupid jokes, more pictures that we'll laugh at later, more late-night talks, more sleepy messages, more "miss na kita," more "kumain ka na," more "ingat ka," more days where we're together and don't even realize that we're creating memories we'll someday miss. I think about our future sometimes, bebe, and when I do, I don't imagine some perfect movie-like life. I imagine something real. I imagine more ordinary mornings, more random trips, more pictures, more inside jokes, more milestones, more birthdays, more celebrations, more difficult days that we get through together, and more nights where we talk about absolutely everything and nothing at the same time. I think about all the memories we haven't made yet, all the places we haven't visited, all the stories we haven't experienced, and all the jokes we haven't created. There are so many versions of our future that we haven't seen yet, and honestly, that makes me excited. I don't want to rush anything. I just want to experience life with you one day at a time. I want to watch us grow, learn, change, and become better versions of ourselves while still finding our way back to each other. I want us to look back someday and laugh about how young we were, how silly we were, and how we used to worry about things that eventually worked themselves out. I want us to have so many memories that we can't possibly remember them all. I want us to have those little "remember when?" moments for years and years. If you're ever having a hard day and you happen to read this, please remember that you don't have to carry everything alone. You don't always have to be strong for everyone else. You don't always have to pretend that you're okay. You are allowed to get tired. You're allowed to have bad days. You're allowed to feel lost, confused, frustrated, sad, or overwhelmed. And when those days come, I hope you remember that you can lean on me. I'm here, bebe. Not only when it's convenient, not only when you're happy, and not only when everything is going perfectly. I'm here for the messy days too. I'm here when you don't know what to say. I'm here when you just need someone to listen. I'm here when you need reassurance. I'm here when you need someone to remind you that you're doing better than you think. I might not always know how to fix everything, but I promise that I'll try to understand. I'll listen. I'll stay. And if all you need is someone beside you while you figure things out, then I'll be that person. I also want you to know how much I believe in you. There might be days when you don't believe in yourself, but I hope you can borrow a little bit of my belief in you. I see the things you sometimes don't see about yourself. I see your effort. I see how much you care. I see the way you keep trying even when you're tired. I see the things you do that other people might overlook. And I hope that whenever you feel like you're not enough, you remember that you don't have to prove your worth to me. You are already important to me simply because you're you. Your value isn't based on how productive you are, how successful you are, or how perfectly you handle everything. You're allowed to be a work in progress. We're both works in progress. And I want to grow with you, not expect you to already have everything figured out. I love knowing that you're the person I want to tell things to. Whenever something funny happens, I want to tell you. Whenever something exciting happens, I want you to know. Whenever something bothers me, you're one of the first people I think about. Whenever I see something that reminds me of you, I automatically think about sending it to you. Sometimes I don't even realize how often you appear in my thoughts until I notice that almost everything somehow reminds me of you. A song, a random place, a stupid joke, a picture, a certain phrase, something we talked about weeks ago—somehow there's always something that brings you back into my mind. And honestly, I don't mind it at all. I like having you in my thoughts. I like remembering you. I like missing you. I like having someone who makes ordinary things feel a little more meaningful. I love you not because everything between us is always perfect, but because even when things aren't perfect, my heart still wants you. I love you because you're someone I want to understand more every day. I love you because I want to keep discovering new things about you even after knowing you for a long time. I love you because you make me laugh in ways I didn't expect. I love you because your presence makes ordinary days feel special. I love you because you make me feel cared for in the smallest ways. I love you because you became someone I can't imagine simply removing from my life. And most of all, I love you because when I think about the person I want beside me through life's little moments, somehow it's always you. I don't know exactly what every day ahead of us will look like, and I don't know what challenges life will eventually give us. I can't promise that everything will always be easy or that we'll never disagree. But I can promise that I'll keep trying. I'll keep learning. I'll keep listening. I'll keep appreciating you. I'll keep remembering why I chose you in the first place. And if I get the chance to choose you again tomorrow, I'll choose you. If I get the chance to choose you the day after that, I'll choose you again. Not because I have to, but because I want to. I want to keep building something with you. I want to keep making memories with you. I want to keep laughing with you. I want to keep annoying you. I want to keep being your person, just as much as I want you to be mine. So if you ever wonder how much you mean to me, I hope you come back to this letter. Read it when you miss me. Read it when you're having a difficult day. Read it when you need a reminder that you're loved. Read it when you feel like nobody notices your efforts. Read it whenever you want to remember how I see you. Because somewhere in this world, there's someone who thinks you're incredibly special. Someone who notices the little things you do. Someone who smiles at random memories of you. Someone who gets excited thinking about the future because you're part of it. Someone who feels lucky simply because they get to know you, love you, and call you bebe. That person is me. And if I could tell you one thing over and over again until you never doubted it, it would be this: **you are loved.** You are appreciated. You are important. You are wanted. You are someone worth choosing. And you never have to wonder whether your presence matters to me because it does. More than you probably realize. You have become part of my everyday life, part of my thoughts, part of my memories, and part of the future I quietly hope for. And even if I don't always say everything perfectly, please know that my feelings for you are real. Sometimes I might not know the right words, sometimes I might express things badly, and sometimes I might get caught up in everyday life, but underneath all of that, there's always this simple truth: **I love you.** I love you on the easy days, and I love you on the complicated ones. I love you when we're laughing until our stomachs hurt, and I love you when we're sitting quietly without saying much. I love you when we're together, and I love you when I miss you from far away. I love you when everything feels perfect, and I love you when things feel messy. I love you when you're being sweet, and I even love you when you're being annoying. HAHAHA. I love all the little versions of you that I get to know. And I hope that no matter how much time passes, we never lose the softness we have for each other. I hope we never stop appreciating the little things. I hope we never stop laughing at stupid jokes. I hope we never stop making memories. I hope we never become too busy to ask each other, "Kumain ka na?" or "Ingat ka." Because maybe those little things are exactly what makes love feel real. So here's to us, bebe. Here's to everything we've already been through, every memory we've already made, every laugh we've shared, every difficult moment we've survived, every picture we've taken, every random conversation we've had, and every little moment that somehow became special simply because it was with you. Here's to all the things we haven't experienced yet. Here's to more pictures, more trips, more inside jokes, more late-night conversations, more random dates, more food trips, more laughter, more hugs, more "miss na kita," more "ingat ka," more "kumain ka na," and so many more ordinary days that I hope we'll someday look back on and realize were actually some of the best days of our lives. I don't need a perfect love story, bebe. I don't need some fairytale where nothing ever goes wrong. I just want ours. Our real, messy, funny, sweet, imperfect, beautiful little story. I want the kind of love where we grow together, where we learn together, where we make mistakes and forgive each other, where we celebrate each other's wins, where we hold each other through difficult days, and where we keep finding reasons to smile even when life isn't perfect. I want a love that feels like home. And somehow, with you, it already does. Thank you for being you. Thank you for staying. Thank you for caring about me in all the little ways. Thank you for making me laugh. Thank you for listening to me. Thank you for putting up with my nonsense. Thank you for making me feel safe enough to be myself. Thank you for all the memories we've already made and for all the memories that are still waiting for us. Thank you for being someone I can look at and think, **"I'm really lucky to have you."** I hope I can make you feel even half as loved and appreciated as you make me feel. I hope I can be your comfort the way you've been mine. I hope I can make your bad days a little lighter, your good days even happier, and your ordinary days a little more special. I love you so much, bebe. More than this letter can explain, more than these paragraphs can hold, and probably more than I know how to put into words. And even after writing all of this, I still feel like there are a thousand more things I could say. Maybe there will always be another letter, another message, another picture, another memory, another random moment where I suddenly think, "I love this person so much." And I hope I never run out of those moments. I hope I never stop finding little reasons to love you. I hope I never stop being excited about making memories with you. I hope that whenever you look at me, you can somehow feel everything that these words are trying to say. So, bebe, if I have to choose you tomorrow, **I will.** If I have to choose you the next day, **I will.** If I have to choose you a thousand times, **I will choose you a thousand times.** Because no matter how many times I get the chance to choose, somehow my heart keeps finding its way back to you. You're the person I want beside me through the good days, the bad days, the boring days, the exciting days, and all the little moments in between. You're the person I want to laugh with, grow with, make memories with, and keep annoying for as long as you'll let me. **I love you, bebe. Today, tomorrow, and all the days after that. And if forever is made up of a million little days, I hope I get to spend as many of those days as possible choosing you, loving you, and reminding you just how much you mean to me.**

With all my heart, always ♡
